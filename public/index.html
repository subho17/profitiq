The Problem

Dealers lose crores.
Silently. Every month.

The leakages are structural — built into the day-to-day operations of every dealership. No ERP catches them. No spreadsheet surfaces them. Until now.

💸

Unauthorised discounts

Sales staff offer discounts beyond approved limits to close deals. No system flags it in real time. Month-end reports arrive too late to recover anything.

₹18,200avg unchecked discount per vehicle, pre-program
🛡️

Insurance commission leakage

Staff arrange insurance through personal agent codes, bypassing the dealership's own insurer relationship and keeping the commission themselves.

67%of 'self-insurance' cases confirmed dealer-arranged on verification calls
💰

Cash not recorded

Customers pay cash at the dealership. The amount recorded in the file is less than what was actually collected. The difference disappears without a trace.

₹85,000avg unrecorded cash found per dealer per month
🔧

Extended warranty fraud

Customer pays for a premium 3-year EW plan. Staff punches a basic 1-year plan in the DMS. The price difference — sometimes ₹12,000 per case — goes into a pocket.

₹96,000EW amount at risk per engagement per month
🏭

Workshop operations fraud

The workshop is where invisible money goes. Spare parts issued for a job but taken home. Cash service jobs completed and collected but never entered in the DMS. Technicians submitting fake warranty claims to the OEM for vehicles that never arrived. Insurance repair jobs where parts are billed and claimed but never fitted. Labour hours inflated on job cards. Consumables — engine oils, batteries, tyres — pilfered in small daily quantities that compound into lakhs across a year. And because no structured audit process exists for workshop operations anywhere in India, it continues undetected.

How It Works

From delivery to insight in under 24 hours

Our Process Consultant is present at the dealership. They audit every delivery file — photos, receipts, documents, all discount heads — via our mobile app in real time.

How We Operate

We place a person.
The platform does the rest.

ProfitIQ is not a self-serve software you deploy and figure out. We assign a dedicated Process Consultant to your dealership — a trained audit professional who shows up every day, works the delivery floor, and operates our platform on your behalf.

You get the human presence of an experienced auditor and the institutional memory of a digital platform — simultaneously.

🧑‍💼
Dedicated Process Consultant at your dealership
Present every working day. Audits every delivery file, photographs the DSR, manages escalations, and coordinates with our back-office team in real time.
🔄
Mandatory rotation every six months
Every Process Consultant is rotated to a different engagement after six months. This eliminates the single biggest risk in any audit program — familiarity and collusion between the auditor and dealer staff. No one stays long enough to be compromised.

The PC operates the ProfitIQ platform
Every file, every photo, every observation is captured through our mobile app. The PC's work is reviewed remotely by our Team Leader. Nothing depends on one person's memory or judgment — the platform enforces the process.
🎯
The deterrence effect starts on day one
When dealer staff know that a trained auditor is present every day and every deal is being independently verified, behaviour changes immediately — before a single finding is raised. This alone recovers significant margin.
